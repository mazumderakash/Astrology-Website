                                  VIMSHOTTARI DASHA

                                  120-Year Bhimshottari Cycle

                                  The Vimshottari Dasha system divides a 120-year cycle into nine planetary Mahadashas. The sequence and traditional themes below are a learning reference, not fixed predictions.

                                  The nine Vimshottari Mahadashas in sequence, their durations, and general traditional themes
                                  Order Mahadasha Duration General interpretation
                                  1Ketu7 yearsOften emphasizes detachment, simplification, research, and inward or spiritual inquiry. Changes may invite a person to reconsider old attachments; the experience depends on Ketu's condition and chart connections.
                                  2Venus (Shukra)20 yearsMay highlight relationships, art, comfort, agreements, resources, and enjoyment. Its constructive expression can support harmony and appreciation; excess may bring overindulgence or attachment.
                                  3Sun (Surya)6 yearsCan bring focus to identity, purpose, confidence, leadership, visibility, and dealings with authority. Themes of recognition and responsibility may be prominent, with care needed around pride or power struggles.
                                  4Moon (Chandra)10 yearsOften draws attention to emotional life, home, family, care, belonging, and public connection. It can be a responsive or changeable period, shaped strongly by the Moon's condition and associations.
                                  5Mars (Mangala)7 yearsMay emphasize initiative, courage, competition, decisive action, technical work, or property matters. Productive effort is possible; impatience, conflict, or avoidable risks may also need management.
                                  6Rahu18 yearsCan intensify ambition, unconventional paths, foreign or unfamiliar settings, technology, and material goals. Rapid change or unusual opportunity may arise, alongside a need to check facts and avoid fixation.
                                  7Jupiter (Guru)16 yearsOften supports themes of learning, teaching, counsel, ethics, faith, and expansion. Growth is most constructive when paired with discernment; excess confidence or overextension can complicate matters.
                                  8Saturn (Shani)19 yearsMay foreground duty, sustained effort, boundaries, service, and long-term structures. Responsibilities can mature through patience and consistency, though delays or heavier workloads may be experienced.
                                  9Mercury (Budha)17 yearsCan bring focus to study, communication, analysis, trade, negotiation, and practical problem-solving. Adaptability is useful; scattered attention, overthinking, or unclear communication may require care.

                                  The durations total 120 years. The sequence begins with the lord of the Moon's birth nakshatra, so the first Mahadasha is commonly experienced only for its remaining balance. Actual interpretations depend on each planet's sign, house, strength, relationships, subperiods, and the full chart; they do not guarantee specific events.

                                    Sa

                                    Saturn

                                    A

                                    Apollo

                                    Below the ring finger; traditionally connected with creativity, appreciation, and expression.

                                    Mercury

                                    Below the little finger; associated with communication, adaptability, and practical skill.

                                    V

                                    Venus

                                    The fleshy area at the thumb base; read symbolically for warmth, affection, and enjoyment.

                                    L

                                    Moon

                                    Along the outer lower palm; traditionally linked with imagination, sensitivity, and travel.

                                    Ma

                                    Mars

                                    Several zones across the palm; associated with courage, restraint, and resilience in traditional readings.
